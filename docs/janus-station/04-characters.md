structure maps cleanly onto a game's cast: antagonists, reference-only figures, retrievable Echoes,
station-resident NPCs, and two crews of persistent companion/rival NPCs the player crew travels
alongside.

## Companion/rival crews

Two crews of persistent NPCs travel through the Fold alongside the player crew — they're written like
player characters: one continuous life, no cutaways, and they don't solve puzzles for the players.

### The Bar Crew

Met each other at the waystation dive before boarding; banded together out of safety, curiosity, and
self-interest — a loose fellowship of convenience, not a team.

| Name | Public role & goal | Secret motive / plot function |

| Jost Aschborn | Berta's kid brother; has a half-formed rumor about a talking skull at the Cairn | Impatient, follows leads alone, trusts the wrong people (especially Rodrick) — generates momentum without solving the treasure plot himself. |

## Protagonist backgrounds

The source LARP gave each of ~14 live players a separate pregenerated character with their own hook —
that's a live-event cast list, not a single-player build. This game has **one controlled protagonist**,
customized at the start of the game by picking a background. A background sets an opening Anomaly Bleed
vision (a short scripted cutscene/vignette establishing why this person is drawn to Janus Station before
they ever board) and a light starting bias toward one quest thread — it should never gate content:
every background can still pursue every quest in
[05-locations-quests-and-items.md](05-locations-quests-and-items.md).

Four curated from the richest source hooks, chosen to each open onto a different kind of play (combat,
investigation, crafting, social):

| Background | Opening vision | Starting bias |
|---|---|---|
| **Void Ranger heir** | Their uncle Dúrion vanished after becoming obsessed with dreams of a station where the dead speak — then they started having the same dream, of a matte-alloy cylinder. | Void Ranger armor plot, the Core Shard |
| **Amnesiac drifter** | Remembers almost nothing — only a deep, ancient voice in dreams: "Come to the Old Oak, I'll show you who you were." A garbled memory of something like a green icon or mascot. | The Old Oak, VERDICT's original friendly-AI branding (see Anomaly Bleed, [02-world-and-verdict.md](02-world-and-verdict.md)) |
| **Investigator** | Word of a station where technology behaves differently — no networks, no cloud-linked augments, only local fabrication — and the dead still talk. Wants to understand the rule the station runs on. | VERDICT, fabrication rules |
| **Salvager-trader** | A black-market broker (Quirin Vogt) has offered good money for anything cursed or strange out of that station — stone, chain, armor, doesn't matter. | Vogt's contract, the Cache |

The remaining source hooks (a warrior called by a vision to the Cache, a colonist scout sent by their
Jarl, a wandering archivist whose keeper-staff rang for Mira and Levin, a Solaris Order commander sent to
prove themselves against Eberwulf, a bio-augmented chemist chasing the Genesis Core, a faith-driven
wanderer sent to cleanse the station) don't need to survive as separate playable slots — fold their
flavor into ambient world content instead: postings at the waystation bar, barks from NPCs who took that
job and didn't come back, or short optional vision fragments unlocked by proximity to the matching
location, regardless of chosen background.

## Companion recruitment

The Bar Crew and Contract Crew above were already written as individual characters with continuous
arcs and their own agendas — that structure needs no rework for single-player. Treat them as a
recruitable companion roster: each can be found and recruited in the world, brought along on
expeditions (recommend a squad cap of 1-2 active companions at a time, in line with genre comparators
like *Prey* and *System Shock*), and has a trust/loyalty state that tracks their personal quest
progress and how the protagonist has treated them. That state is what determines who shows up for the
protagonist in the finale — see
[06-endings-and-implementation-handoff.md](06-endings-and-implementation-handoff.md).
