Maintenance log, day forty one. Whoever picks this up after me: the panel by the vault has been on its factory number since the day it was fitted, and I have stopped asking. If a number is written down somewhere in this hall, it is written down because the panel would not let anyone in otherwise. Read what you find. Do not prop the doors. And if the slide door on the north wall closes behind you, it does that on its own, it is not the station being clever. Vance, out.
